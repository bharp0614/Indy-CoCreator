# Trust Checklist Block

## H2
Hire a Restoration Company That Checks Every Box

## Copy
Given the high cost of water, fire, smoke, soot, and ash restoration, choosing the right contractor matters. Before work begins, ask about references, licensing where required, liability insurance, written estimates, insurance documentation, and restoration training.

## Checklist
- References from past customers
- Licensing where required
- Bonding and liability insurance
- Written estimate before work begins
- Insurance documentation support
- Restoration certifications or training
- Recognized restoration practices
