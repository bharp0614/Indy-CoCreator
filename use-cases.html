We acquire and redevelop residential properties throughout Central Indiana. There is no obligation to accept an offer.

How Our Acquisition Process Works

Our goal is to make the transition of your property as straightforward as possible. We do not use automated calculators or high-pressure sales tactics.

  1. Initial Contact: Submit your property details through our secure form.
  2. Information Review: We evaluate recent comparable sales in the neighborhood.
  3. On-Site Walkthrough: We schedule a brief, in-person walkthrough to assess necessary repairs.
  4. Written Offer: You receive a clear, written offer based on our renovation scope.
  5. Title & Closing: We handle the transaction through a reputable, local title company.

How We Calculate Offers

Our cash offers are grounded in empirical market realities. We review:

  • Current property condition and necessary cosmetic or structural updates.
  • Cost of materials and direct renovation team labor rates.
  • Recent comparable property sales in the immediate neighborhood.
  • Holding and transaction costs during the renovation period.
  • Local title company and closing expenses.
