GROUP A — 24/7 EMERGENCY SERVICE FUNNEL
Emergency Restoration / Water Damage / Fire Damage / Smoke / Soot / Ash

PURPOSE
This folder defines the funnel needs, branding direction, CSS tokens, and builder notes for Group A emergency-service websites and landing pages.

GROUP A DEFINITION
Group A is for urgent services where the customer has a problem happening right now.

PRIMARY CUSTOMER STATE
- Stressed
- Time-sensitive
- Worried about damage getting worse
- Likely searching on mobile
- Wants proof the company can respond fast
- Needs a phone number immediately
- Needs trust signals before calling

PRIMARY CTA
Call Now

SECONDARY CTA
Request a Written Estimate

BEST CTA FORMAT
Call 317-627-5668

PRIMARY PAGE GOAL
Move the visitor from panic to action:
1. Confirm the emergency problem
2. Show 24/7 availability
3. Explain why fast action matters
4. Build trust quickly
5. Push click-to-call
6. Offer estimate / insurance documentation support

GROUP A CORE MESSAGE
The first 24 to 48 hours are critical. Fast response helps reduce further damage, structural compromise, mold risk, smoke residue staining, soot contamination, ash damage, and higher restoration costs.

BEST SERVICES FOR THIS GROUP
- Water damage restoration
- Fire damage restoration
- Smoke cleanup
- Soot cleanup
- Ash cleanup
- Flood cleanup
- Emergency water extraction
- Structural drying
- Insurance documentation support

LOCAL SEO FOCUS
Indianapolis, Indiana and surrounding Central Indiana cities.

DESIGN DIRECTION
Urgent but calm.
Professional, not scary.
High contrast, fast readability, strong phone CTA, trust-first layout.
