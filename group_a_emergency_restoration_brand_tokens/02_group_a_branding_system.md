# Group A Branding System
## Emergency Restoration / Urgent Service Websites

## Brand Position
**Fast, calm, qualified emergency response.**

Group A branding should communicate urgency without making the visitor feel more panicked. The visual system should feel professional, stable, and capable.

## Visual Direction
- Clean restoration-company look
- High contrast
- Strong CTA color
- Calm neutral background
- Trust-building dark header
- Emergency accent used sparingly
- Service cards that are easy to scan
- Phone number always visible

## Brand Personality
- Immediate
- Prepared
- Local
- Professional
- Direct
- Trustworthy
- Documentation-aware
- Insurance-aware

## Color Strategy

### Primary Base
Use a deep charcoal/navy tone for trust, seriousness, and professional service.

### Emergency Accent
Use a controlled red/orange accent for urgent CTAs and emergency labels.

### Restoration Neutral
Use warm off-white, soft gray, and clean white for readability.

### Service Support Colors
Use small accent colors to distinguish service types:
- Water: blue
- Fire: ember orange
- Smoke/Soot: charcoal gray
- Ash: cool gray
- Insurance/Estimate: green or blue trust accent

## Suggested Font Direction
Use strong, readable sans-serif fonts.

Best types:
- Inter
- Manrope
- Source Sans 3
- IBM Plex Sans
- System UI

## Layout Rules

### Header
Must include:
- Logo / business name
- Service area
- Phone number
- Emergency CTA button

### Hero
Must include:
- 24/7 emergency label
- Damage types handled
- Indianapolis / Central Indiana
- First 24–48 hours urgency
- Phone CTA
- Written estimate CTA

### Trust Bar
Use short trust items:
- 24/7 Response
- Water Extraction
- Fire Cleanup
- Smoke & Soot Removal
- Written Estimates
- Insurance Support

### Service Cards
Each card should answer:
- What problem is handled?
- Why does it matter now?
- What should the customer do?

### Sticky Mobile CTA
Group A should use a sticky mobile call bar:
**Call 317-627-5668**

## Image Direction
Use realistic images, not overly polished stock photos.

Best image types:
- Water extraction equipment
- Restoration technician in protective gear
- Smoke/soot cleanup
- Fire-damaged room
- Structural drying equipment
- Technician documenting damage
- Before/after restoration work
- Local service truck or crew if available

## Icon Direction
Use simple line icons or filled utility icons:
- Phone
- Clock / 24/7
- Water drop
- Flame
- Shield
- Clipboard
- Map pin
- Home
- Building
- Checkmark

## Brand Warnings
Do not claim:
- Licensed
- Insured
- Bonded
- Certified
- IICRC-certified
- RIA-certified
- 5-star rated
- Free estimates
- Works with all insurance companies

Unless the company has verified those claims.

## Safe Trust Language
- Ask about licensing, insurance, certifications, and written estimates.
- Written estimates available.
- Insurance documentation support available.
- Damage documentation support available.
- Serving Indianapolis and Central Indiana.
- Emergency restoration help available.
