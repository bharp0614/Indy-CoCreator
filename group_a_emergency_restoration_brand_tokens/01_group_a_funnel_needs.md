# Group A Funnel Needs — 24/7 Emergency Services

## Group Name
**Group A — 24/7 Emergency Service**

## Best Fit Industries
- Water damage restoration
- Fire damage restoration
- Smoke and soot cleanup
- Mold remediation
- Emergency HVAC repair
- Emergency plumbing
- Locksmith services
- Storm damage response
- Biohazard cleanup
- Emergency electrical repair

## Customer Mindset
The visitor is not casually researching. They likely have an active problem happening now.

They need:
- A phone number immediately
- Proof the company handles their exact emergency
- Fast reassurance
- Clear service area
- Trust signals
- Estimate or insurance help
- A simple next step

## Restoration-Specific Customer Need
For water, fire, smoke, soot, and ash restoration, the customer is worried about:
- Water spreading behind walls or under floors
- Mold or rot
- Structural damage
- Smoke odor
- Soot staining
- Ash contamination
- Insurance claim confusion
- Cleanup cost
- Whether the property is safe

## Conversion Priorities

### Priority 1 — Immediate Call
The phone CTA should appear:
- In the top header
- In the hero
- After the emergency problem section
- After the services section
- In the sticky mobile footer
- In the final CTA section

### Priority 2 — Trust Before Detail
Emergency visitors do not need long explanations first. They need to know:
- You respond fast
- You handle the damage type
- You serve their city
- You can provide an estimate
- You can support documentation/insurance needs

### Priority 3 — Local Confirmation
Use Indianapolis and Central Indiana early on the page.

Example:
**Emergency restoration services in Indianapolis and surrounding Central Indiana communities.**

### Priority 4 — Insurance / Estimate Support
The page should state:
- Written estimates available
- Damage documentation support
- Photos and documentation support
- Insurance communication support where applicable

## Group A Page Structure

1. Emergency header with phone CTA
2. Hero with urgent problem + local service area
3. Trust bar
4. First 24–48 hours urgency section
5. Service cards
6. Insurance / estimate support section
7. Contractor checklist section
8. Service area section
9. Final CTA

## Best Tone
- Clear
- Fast
- Reassuring
- Professional
- Direct
- Calm under pressure

## Avoid
- Cute language
- Long intros
- Over-designed layouts
- Low contrast
- Hidden phone number
- Claims that are not verified
- Too much text before the CTA
