The important distinction is that these are seven different homepage schemes. They can share component code—such as cards, reviews, FAQs, forms and service areas—but the component order and conversion logic change according to how customers buy that service.











These are the default section titles and homepage forms for each master template. The wording can change by industry through JSON, but the structure stays consistent.

Group A

Home -> section 1 -> hero -> form

Desktop hero: about 500–600px tall
Layout: 70% content/image, 30% form
Desktop image: 2000 × 1000px, WebP
Mobile image: 900 × 1200px
Tablet image:
Overlay: dark left gradient for text; light bottom shadow
Headline: bold condensed font, around 64–80px desktop
Body font: clean sans-serif, around 18–22px
Form: 340–400px wide
Fields: name, phone, issue, ZIP/address
Button: Request Service
Mobile: stack content and form; keep sticky Call Now button










Subpage hero variation
Height: 320–420px desktop
Image: 1600 × 700px, WebP
Layout: 55% text, 45% image focus
Overlay: dark left gradient
Breadcrumb: Home → Services → Current Service
Headline: service or project name, 48–64px
Supporting text: one short sentence
Primary CTA: Call Now or Request Estimate
Secondary CTA: View All Services
Trust line: licensed, local, response time, or warranty
Form: no full form in hero
Mobile: 400–550px; text and CTA over image
Below hero: service details or project information immediately

## 7. Light Retail / Mobile Diagnostic

**Section titles**

1. Hero: **Convenient Service That Comes to You**
2. Trust bar: no title
3. Services: **Choose the Service You Need**
4. Problem selector: **What Can We Help With?**
5. Process: **Book Your Service in Three Simple Steps**
6. Benefits: **Why Customers Choose Mobile Service**
7. Pricing: **Straightforward Estimates Before Work Begins**
8. Service area: **Check Our Mobile Service Area**
9. Reviews: **What Local Customers Say**
10. FAQ: **Booking and Service Questions**
11. Final CTA: **Ready to Schedule Your Service?**

**Homepage form: `MobileServiceRequestForm`**

* Name
* Phone
* Service ZIP code
* Service needed
* Vehicle, appliance or item details
* Preferred service date

Button: **Request Service**

The exact details field changes by industry:

* Auto glass: year, make, model and damaged glass
* Mobile mechanic: year, make, model and problem
* Appliance repair: appliance type, brand and problem
* Junk removal: items and approximate amount

These are the **homepage forms only**. Contact forms, complete estimate forms, job applications and insurance-detail forms belong on their appropriate internal pages.
