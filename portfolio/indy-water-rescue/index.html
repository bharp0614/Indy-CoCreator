Why timing matters

The first 24 to 48 hours are critical

  1. 1
    First hours

    Water wicks up drywall, soaks carpet pad and spreads under flooring.

  2. 2
    24–48 hours

    Mold can begin to grow on wet materials, per EPA guidance.

  3. 3
    After 48 hours

    Floors warp, drywall weakens and repairs grow from drying to rebuilding.
